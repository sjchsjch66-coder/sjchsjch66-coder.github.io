PRICE & ADS

Price & ads

Good to know before you start.

Price
Free to play. No Ads and the Halloween (5 pictures) and Christmas (10 pictures) packs are optional one-time purchases. No subscriptions.
Ads
No ads while you are coloring. A full-screen ad appears once every 3 finished pictures, after you tap a button on the finished screen.
Videos
Watch an optional video to unlock a new picture early or to fill all cells of one color at once. With No Ads, you can use these without videos.

Q&A

FAQ

Need more help? Visit Support.

Is it free?

Yes, it is free to play with ads. No Ads and picture packs are one-time purchases.

What if I tap the wrong cell?

Nothing happens. Cells with a different number simply don't fill.

Can I move my pictures to a new iPhone?

Yes. Your pictures are saved to iCloud and come back with the same Apple Account.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. It is available on iPhone (iOS 15 or later).
