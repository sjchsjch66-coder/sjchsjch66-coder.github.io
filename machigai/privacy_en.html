Privacy Policy | Pixel Spot the Difference

CHIHIRO SHIRATA ("we") explains below how user information is handled in the smartphone game "Pixel Spot the Difference" (the "App"). This page is the English version of our Japanese privacy policy for the App (ぽことまちがいさがし).

1. Information we collect

We do not collect information that identifies you, such as your name, email address, phone number, address, or photos. The App does not send your game data to our servers.

2. Game data

Your progress and settings are stored on your device. So that you can keep your data when you delete the App or change devices, it is also stored in your own iCloud account (an Apple service). Data in iCloud is managed by Apple; we cannot see or receive it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows two kinds of ads. It does not show banner ads.

  • Video ads: shown only when you choose "Hint +1 (Watch Video)" or "+30s & Continue (Watch Video)" (up to 10 times a day).
  • Full-screen ads: shown occasionally after you clear a puzzle (none for the first 5 puzzles; then once every 5 or 3 clears), after you tap a button on the clear screen. They never appear while you are playing. Buying the in-app purchase "Remove Ads" stops them.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. If your device's language is not Japanese, or your App Store country or region is not Japan, the App:

  • asks Google to treat its ad requests as child-directed under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), so Google does not show interest-based (personalized) ads or remarketing ads; and
  • does not show the iOS "Allow tracking?" prompt, so your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not made available for advertising.

To show ads, Google may still collect and use information such as your approximate location estimated from your IP address, your device type and OS version, ad views and taps, and information about how the App runs.

For how Google handles information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App schedules game reminders on your device. No information is sent to us for notifications.

5. In-app purchases

Payments are processed by Apple Inc. through the App Store. We never receive your payment details such as credit card numbers. To process purchases, we may store the transaction number sent by Apple in your game data.

6. Information you send us

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we do not share them with third parties except as required by law.

7. Children

We do not collect personal information such as names or email addresses from anyone, including children. As described in section 3, outside Japan the App treats ad requests as child-directed and does not ask for tracking permission.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the update on this page.
