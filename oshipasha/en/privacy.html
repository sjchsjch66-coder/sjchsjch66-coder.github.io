Privacy Policy | Oshi & Me

CHIHIRO SHIRATA (“we”) sets out the following policy on how the smartphone camera app “Oshi & Me” (“the app”) handles information about its users.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any information that can identify you, such as your name, email address, phone number, address, contacts or photos. We do not send your app data to any server of ours.

2. Camera, photos and app data

The camera image, your photos, and face and body detection are all processed only on your device. None of this is sent off your device, either to us or to any third party. A photo is passed to your Photos app, or to an app you choose, only when you tap “Save to Photos” or “Share”. The faves, frames and settings you create are saved on your device. So that you can keep your data when you delete the app or change phones, it is also saved in your own iCloud (a service of Apple). Data saved in iCloud is managed by Apple, and we cannot see or receive it.

Face and body detection is used only to find where things are in the photo. It is never used to identify anyone, it is not saved, and it is not used for ads.

To set a spending limit for minors, the app asks for your age group (15 or under, 16 to 19, or 20 and over) the first time you make a purchase. This age group and the amount spent that month are also saved only on your device and in your own iCloud.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

This app shows the following ads. It does not show banner ads.

  • Video ads: shown only when you tap “Watch a video” yourself (up to 10 times a day).
  • Full-screen ads: shown after you finish saving to Photos or sharing, about once every 3 times (never during the first 24 hours after installing, and never for your first 3 saves). They are never shown while you are taking or finishing a photo. Buying the in-app purchase “Remove Ads” turns them off.

Ads are shown using Google AdMob, an ad service of Google LLC. To show ads, Google may collect and use information such as:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you choose “Allow” for tracking
  • Your approximate region, estimated from your IP address
  • Device type and OS version, records of ads shown and tapped, and information about how the app runs

When you open the app, your iPhone asks “Allow … to track your activity?”. Even if you do not allow it, you can still watch video ads and get rewards. You can change this at any time in your iPhone’s Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

For how Google handles information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. In-app purchases

Payments for in-app purchases are processed by Apple Inc. on the App Store. We never receive your payment information, such as credit card numbers. To process purchases, we may save the transaction number sent by Apple in the app data.

5. Information you send us

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we do not give them to any third party unless required by law.

“Report an Ad” (our feedback form) in the app’s Settings uses Google Forms, a service of Google LLC. What you send in the form is stored on Google’s service, and we read it to reply to you and to improve the app. You don’t need to write your name or contact details in the form. For how Google handles information, see Google’s Privacy Policy.

6. How to delete your data

  • Tap “Delete data” in the app’s Settings to delete your faves, outfits and frames both on your device and in your own iCloud (purchase records, your age group and the volume setting are kept).
  • Deleting the app deletes the data on your device, but the copy saved in iCloud remains. To delete everything, first tap “Delete data”, then delete the app.
  • Photos you saved to your Photos app can be deleted in the Photos app.
  • If you would like us to delete something you sent us by email or through the form, please email us.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will announce it on this page.
