In-app purchases and ads

Frames
You can make up to 5 frames. Want more? Add +5, or Lots of Frames (up to 100).
Packs
Decoration packs are yours to keep after one purchase. Changing phones? Use Restore Purchases.
Ads
Locked stickers unlock one at a time when you watch a video ad. An ad sometimes shows after you save a photo. Buy Remove Ads to turn them off.

FAQ

Need more help? Visit Support.

Do I have to bring my plushie every time?

No. Once you shoot and cut out your fave at home, they stay in the app. When you're out, you can bring them into your camera shot even if they're not with you.

Does it work with acrylic stands and photocards?

Yes. Plushies, acrylic stands, photocards, figures and pin badges can all be cut out in their own shape.

Where are my photos saved?

Tap Save to Photos to save them to your iPhone's Photos app. Camera images and photos never leave your device.
